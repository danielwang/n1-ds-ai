# Voice and tone

Status: no approved N1 voice-and-tone or terminology guide was found. Existing copy and proposed guidance are separated below. Reviewed 15 October 2026.

## Human-provided context

N1 serves higher education institutions and multiple audiences: students, academic staff, agent creators, administrators, and other institutional users. The supplied product brief asks for clear and trustworthy experiences. It does not define audience-specific tone, reading level, spelling locale, or approved terminology.

## Existing source copy

The authored [Design for Real Work principle](design-principles.md#figma-authored-design-for-real-work) centres meaningful work and reduced effort. It is product philosophy, not a complete writing guide.

[Button](../components/button.md), [TextField](../components/input.md), and [Dialog](../components/dialog.md) preserve text samples such as action labels and field labels. These are source fixtures. They do not establish approved production copy, case conventions, terminology, or tone. No existing source wording has been rewritten into a new standard.

## Proposed writing guidance — not approved N1 policy

- Use clear task language suited to the user's institutional context; explain unfamiliar AI terminology when it is necessary.
- Make actions and recovery steps understandable. For an error, describe what is known and the available next step.
- Distinguish AI-generated suggestions, uncertainty, and verified outcomes. Avoid wording that implies certainty or institutional authority without evidence.
- Adapt detail to the task and audience while keeping terminology consistent within a workflow.

These are proposed UX writing directions derived from the supplied goals. They are not Figma-derived rules or approved N1 copy. They should not automatically replace existing product text.
