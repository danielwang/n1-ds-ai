## Human-provided product direction

The product brief supplied for this synthesis identifies N1 as a higher education AI platform serving students, academic staff, agent creators, administrators, and other institutional users. It calls for clarity, accessibility, consistency, and trustworthiness. These goals are recorded as user-provided direction; they do not supply detailed policies, acceptance criteria, or an institutional approval record.

## Figma-authored: Design for Real Work

Original text, preserved from component `Design Principle`, node `6849:1347`; title node `6849:1349`, body node `6849:1350`:

> Students and uni staff use nebulaONE to accomplish meaningful work, not to explore technology.
>
> Every workflow should minimise effort and maximise progress.
>
> Measure success by:
> - Time saved
> - Tasks completed
> - Reduced cognitive load
> - Not by clicks or prompts.

## Proposed application guidance — not approved N1 policy

- Identify the institutional user's task and a meaningful completion outcome before selecting a workflow.
- Evaluate a change against effort, progress, and the source's stated success measures; document the evaluation method rather than substituting activity counts.

These are suggested applications of the authored principle, not additional Figma rules or approved acceptance criteria.
